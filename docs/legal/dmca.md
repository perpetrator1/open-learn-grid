# DMCA Takedown Policy

## Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe material on this Open Learn Grid instance infringes your copyright, please notify the instance administrator.

## Required Information

A valid DMCA notice must include:

1. **Identification of the copyrighted work** — describe the work you claim is infringed
2. **Identification of the infringing material** — the URL of the specific material on our instance
3. **Contact information** — your name, address, phone number, and email
4. **Good faith statement** — "I have a good faith belief that the use of the copyrighted material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law"
5. **Accuracy statement** — "The information in this notification is accurate, and I am the copyright owner or authorised to act on behalf of the copyright owner"
6. **Signature** — your physical or electronic signature

## Submit a Notice

Send your notice to the email address on the About page. We will acknowledge receipt within 3 business days.

## Response Process

Upon receiving a valid notice, we will:

1. Remove the identified material promptly (within 2 business days)
2. Notify the user who uploaded the material
3. Provide the user an opportunity to submit a counter-notice

## Counter-Notice

If you believe material was removed in error, contact us with a counter-notice including:

1. Identification of the removed material
2. Statement under penalty of perjury that removal was a mistake
3. Your contact information and consent to jurisdiction

## Repeat Infringers

Users who repeatedly infringe copyright will have their accounts terminated.
