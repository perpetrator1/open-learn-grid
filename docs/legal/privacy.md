# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: March 2026*

---

## 1. What Data We Collect

When you use this service, we collect:

- **Account information:** username, email address, display name, institution
- **Usage data:** materials you upload, download, and view; verification actions
- **Technical data:** IP address (stored in audit logs, deleted after 90 days), browser user agent, request timestamps

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To provide the service and authenticate your account
- To send transactional emails (password reset, notifications)
- To enforce our Terms of Service and investigate abuse
- To maintain security audit logs

We do **not** sell your data to third parties.

## 3. Federation

If this instance is part of the Open Learn Grid federation network:

- Your **username** (not email) may be included in material metadata shared with trusted instances
- No passwords, email addresses, or IP addresses are ever shared with federated instances

## 4. Cookies

We use session cookies (HTTP-only, Secure) for authentication. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

## 5. Data Retention

- Account data: retained while your account is active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- Uploaded materials: retained until you delete them or your account is deleted
- Audit logs: automatically deleted after 90 days
- Notifications: automatically deleted after 30 days

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data — request an export via your profile settings
- **Rectify** your data — update your account in profile settings
- **Erase** your data — request account deletion by contacting the administrator
- **Portability** — download your uploaded materials at any time

To exercise these rights, contact the administrator at the email on the About page.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices:
- HTTPS for all data in transit
- Bcrypt password hashing
- JWT tokens with short expiry
- Rate limiting on authentication endpoints

## 8. Changes

This policy may be updated. We will notify registered users of material changes.

## 9. Contact

Data protection inquiries: contact the instance administrator.
